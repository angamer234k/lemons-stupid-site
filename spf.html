Please don't drop forum links in #community

(or any other forum channel, really)

You might think you're being helpful or "networking", but when you post a link to your own forum inside someone else's community space — especially in channels like #community on Discord or Roblox Catweb — it's just spam.

  • It hijacks a conversation that isn't about your project
  • It shows zero respect for the community you're posting in
  • It almost never leads to genuine engagement — only annoyance
  • It can get you muted, banned, or quietly ignored forever

👉 What to do instead

  • Join the community and actually contribute to discussions
  • If there's a dedicated #promo or #self-plug channel, use it once — politely
  • Let people discover your forum naturally through your helpfulness and reputation
  • Build real connections — nobody clicks a random link from a stranger

This applies everywhere, but it's especially painful in Discord servers and the Roblox game Catweb, where #community forums are meant for conversation, not advertising.

A community channel is not your billboard. 🛑

Inspired by the spirit of nohello.net. Just be a decent human — don't spam your links.
