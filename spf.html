Stop Posting Forum Links

(SPF for short)

You've seen it. Someone joins a community and within minutes drops a link to their own forum, Discord server, or Roblox group. Often in a #community channel where people are actually talking. It's not networking — it's spam.

This happens everywhere, but especially in Discord servers and the CatWeb community. CatWeb's own forums are meant for its community; hijacking them with links to an external forum is disrespectful and annoying.

Here's what it looks like in practice:

See the difference? In the second example, the person became a real part of the community first. They contributed value, made connections, and someone else asked them about their forum. That's how real growth happens.

Every community is someone's home on the internet. Dropping unsolicited links is like walking into a dinner party and handing out flyers for your own event down the street. Use dedicated #promo channels if they exist — once, politely — and let your reputation do the rest.

A community channel is not your billboard. 🛑
