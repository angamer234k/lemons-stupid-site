⚙️ How does Honeypot work?

A simple but powerful defence against spam bots.
1

Invite & set up /honeypot

Invite the bot with Ban Members and Manage Channels permissions. The bot will create a #honeypot channel automatically, or you can set it up yourself with the /honeypot slash command.[reference:0]

2

Choose your action kick / ban

Configure the admin log channel and choose whether the bot should kick (softban) or ban offenders. The default is a softban, which deletes their recent messages.[reference:1][reference:2]

3

Position the trap wisely

Place your honeypot channel near the top of your server list — many spam bots target the first few channels they see.[reference:3] You can also rename it to something less predictable, like #pls-dont-chat-here, to avoid automated blacklists.[reference:4]

4

Bot takes immediate action

As soon as someone posts in the honeypot channel, the bot acts instantly — removing the user and deleting their messages before they can spread spam further.[reference:5][reference:6] All actions are logged in your admin channel.

5

Optional experiments pro

Enable extra features like Channel Warmer (keeps the channel active), Random Channel Name (changes daily), Timeout First (1hr timeout before action), or Many Honeypots (multiple trap channels).[reference:7]

Pro tip: Make sure the bot’s highest role is above any self‑assignable roles so it has the authority to remove problematic accounts.[reference:8][reference:9]

❓ Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know about Honeypot.

What is Honeypot Discord Bot?

Honeypot is a spam‑prevention bot that automatically catches and removes spam bots by monitoring a dedicated #honeypot channel.[reference:10][reference:11] It's designed to stop the "flood all channels" style of spam attack.

How do I add Honeypot to my server?

Click the “Add to server” button on this site, then select your server and approve the requested permissions (Ban Members, Manage Channels, etc.).[reference:12] The bot will create a #honeypot channel on join.[reference:13]

What commands does Honeypot have?

Honeypot uses two main slash commands: /honeypot to configure the honeypot channel and its settings, and /honeypot-messages to customise the messages the bot sends.[reference:14]

What happens when someone posts in the honeypot channel?

The bot acts immediately — the user is banned or kicked (softban), their messages are deleted, and the action is logged in your admin channel.[reference:15][reference:16] This happens before they can spread spam further.

Can I change the honeypot channel name?

Yes! You can rename the trap channel to anything you like. In fact, it's recommended to use a less predictable name (like #pls-dont-chat-here) to avoid automated bots that blacklist the word “honeypot”.[reference:17] You can also enable the Random Channel Name experiment to change it automatically every day.[reference:18]

Is Honeypot open source?

Yes. The bot is open source and available on GitHub. You can view the code, contribute, or self‑host if you prefer.[reference:19][reference:20]

How many servers use Honeypot?

Honeypot is trusted by over 38,600 Discord servers and continues to grow.[reference:21]

What permissions does the bot need?

The bot requires Ban Members, Manage Channels, Send Messages, Manage Messages (for deletion), and Read Message History.[reference:22][reference:23] Make sure its role is ranked above standard member roles.[reference:24]
